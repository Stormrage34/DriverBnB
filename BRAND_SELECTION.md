# Brand Selection and Preliminary Clearance

**Date:** 13 November 2026
**Decision:** Adopt **Milevana** as the new product brand.
**Positioning:** Milevana — a driver-first professional-driver marketplace.
**Supporting line:** Built around drivers.
**Core promise:** Drivers choose. Employers provide transparent information.

This is a preliminary naming and register search, not legal clearance. No trademark was filed,
no domain was purchased, and no social handle was reserved.

## 1. Method

Twenty candidates were scored 1–5 on eight criteria, equally weighted (maximum 40):

1. Distinctiveness.
2. Driver-first positioning.
3. Memorability.
4. Pronunciation and spelling across European languages.
5. Expansion beyond a single job board.
6. Preliminary trademark risk (5 = lowest observed risk).
7. Domain and commercial-use position.
8. Professional B2B2C fit.

Candidates with a material same-sector commercial conflict or a directly conflicting EU/national
mark were rejected before the shortlist, even where the name scored well.

## 5. Winner rationale

Milevana is the only shortlisted name that combines:

- a coined, distinctive word with no exact TMview hit;
- no active same-sector commercial use found;
- a `.com` that appeared unregistered at the search date;
- easy Latin-script pronunciation and spelling for English, German, Dutch, Bulgarian and the
  Scandinavian languages;
- enough breadth to cover jobs, employer information, reviews and a wider driver ecosystem without
  becoming a generic job-board name.

The name does not itself say “driver”. That is deliberate: the positioning line carries the meaning,
while the brand stays distinctive and registrable. “Driver-first” remains a descriptor and tagline,
not the brand.

## 6. Risks and limits

- **No legal clearance.** This is a register and commercial-use search, not a freedom-to-operate
  opinion. A qualified EU trademark attorney must run a similarity search in Classes 35 and 42
  (and Class 9 only if a downloadable application is actually planned).
- **EUIPO eSearch was unavailable.** The EUIPO endpoint redirected to its WAF error page during this
  session; TMview was used as the cross-register source. TMview aggregates EUIPO and national data,
  but the official EUIPO file should be pulled by counsel.
- **WIPO Global Brand Database was bot-challenged.** TMview all-office results include international
  registrations; a direct WIPO check remains part of formal clearance.
- **Unpublished applications and unregistered use are invisible.** The French `MILEVANA` holding
  company is a company-name risk to review, not a trademark clearance.
- **Domains and handles are not secured.** A 404 from the `.com` registry is evidence of
  non-registration at that moment, not a reservation. No purchase was made.
- **The Swedish DRIVERS FIRST conflict is avoided, not resolved.** EUTM application `019040185`
  (figurative, class 39, status Ended) and the Swedish operator remain relevant to the old brand.
  The new brand must not use “Drivers First” as a trademark or domain.
- **No claim of availability.** Say “no published exact mark found on the search date”, never
  “trademark-cleared”.

## 7. Next actions before brand investment

1. Instruct counsel to search Milevana in Classes 35 and 42, including phonetic and visual
   similarity, EUIPO, WIPO, Sweden, Germany, Benelux, France and the UK.
2. Confirm the French company’s activities and any earlier unregistered use.
3. Register `milevana.com` and the relevant `.eu`/national domains only after counsel’s opinion.
4. Reserve social handles only after the name is approved.
5. Do not print merchandise, signage or paid media before clearance.
