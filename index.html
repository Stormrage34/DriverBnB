Questions drivers ask

Is DriverCompass a recruitment agency?

No. We publish the conditions, record what drivers report and carry the enquiry. The employment discussion happens directly between driver and employer.

Can an employer pay for better reviews?

No. A subscription buys listing tools. It moves no review, score, check or ranking, and a paid listing is never marked verified.

What does it cost a driver?

Nothing. Reading records and reviews, and contacting employers through the platform, are free.

Do I have to upload my CV or my documents?

No. Profiles work without documents. Optional checks are never public, and you can remove them.

What happens to my reviews if I hide my profile?

They stay published: hiding a profile hides you, not what you reported. You choose what is shown next to a review.
