## 3. Hero copy

**Headline:** Know the employer. Choose your next move.

**Subheadline:** Compare transport employers, read what drivers who worked there reported, and contact the company directly. Free for drivers.

**Primary action:** Create your driver profile
**Secondary action:** Find employers
**Proof line:** Pilot build: company profiles are demo data, and no vacancies are live yet.

## 4. Benefits (outcome driven)

1. **See the conditions before you apply.** Pay, allowances, rotation, home time, accommodation and loading duties are fields, not paragraphs.
2. **Read drivers who were actually there.** Every review says what was verified and what was not.
3. **Show what you can do.** Licences, Code 95, ADR classes, routes and equipment in one profile you control.
4. **Write to the employer yourself.** No agency, no allocation, no ranking.
5. **Nothing is for sale.** Payment never moves a review, a score or a verification.

## 5. How it works (three steps for the page, four in the build)

1. **Search employers, not adverts.** Filter by country, pay, rotation and accommodation.
2. **Read the record and the reviews.** Each field and each review carries its evidence.
3. **Contact the company directly.** You choose what to share and when.

## 6. FAQ

**Is DriverCompass a recruitment agency?** No. It publishes conditions, records experiences and carries the enquiry. Employment discussions happen directly with the employer.

**Can employers pay for better reviews?** No. A subscription buys listing tools. It does not move a review, a score, a verification or a ranking.

**How is a review verified?** The platform confirms the employer, the relationship and the period. It does not confirm the opinion, and it never publishes the driver's identity to do it.

**What happens if I hide my profile?** Your reviews stay published. Hiding a profile hides you, not what you reported.

**Do I have to upload my CV or documents?** No. A profile works without documents. Document checks are optional and never public.

**What does it cost a driver?** Nothing. Creating a profile, reading records and contacting employers through the platform are free.
